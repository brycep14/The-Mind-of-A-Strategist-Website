About Me

Bryce Peterson

I’m Bryce Peterson, a fan of sports, an enjoyer of art, a lover of dinosaurs, and an aspiring advertising strategist.

I’m currently studying advertising at Brigham Young University and part of the prestigious BYU AdLab.

I enjoy doing new activities, exploring new hobbies, and learning new fun facts. My brain seems to work best when my hands are given something to do.

Despite my 6’5” height now, I was too short during my school years to play basketball (my dream sport) so I settled for cross country and track. Running taught me a lot about discipline, grit, and determination, but nowadays I just use it to get into shape for intramural basketball (which is my NBA finals these days).

Born in Oklahoma, I hold strong allegiance to Oklahoma City Thunder basketball, and to the Oklahoma and Oklahoma State University teams (my parents were a modern day Romeo and Juliet if you know what Bedlam is).

Brigham Young University has captured my heart now though. I have fallen in love with its mountains (perfect for close skiing), its people, and its cool temperatures (cold > hot).

I hope to learn everything I can about whatever subjects I can get my hand on in hopes of creating advertising campaigns that will inspire positive actions beyond brand sales (though those are important too).

If you want to talk or play sports, have a hobby you want someone to try with you, or just want to make something inspiring, please hit me up!
